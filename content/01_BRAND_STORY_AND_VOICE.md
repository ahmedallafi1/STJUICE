# ST. JUICE — Brand Story and Voice

## Approved public story

ST. JUICE started with one simple idea: fresh and indulgent should belong in the same place.

We make bright juices, layered smoothies and fruit-forward bowls for the everyday. Then we turn up the mood with warm crepes, chocolate-dipped fruit, mini pancakes, pistachio finishes and boxes made to share.

Our first home is at 11 S Vandeventer Ave in St. Louis, built for quick morning pickup, an afternoon reset, a late-night craving or the whole table. Every order should feel clear, customizable and worth coming back for.

**Your mood, made fresh.**

## Message pillars

### Fresh energy

Lead with fruit, made-to-order preparation and cold refreshment without unsupported wellness claims.

### Sweet mood

Show texture, warmth, drizzle and crunch while keeping the menu organized and the copy controlled.

### Made your way

Build Your Mood turns customization into a guided choice with live pricing and allergen impact.

### Better together

Flights, date boxes, study boxes, party boxes and catering make sharing a core behavior.

### St. Louis first

The location and local audience are part of the story. Avoid invented history, “best in St. Louis” claims or implied university affiliation.

## Writing rules

- Lead with taste, texture or occasion.
- Prefer active verbs: pressed, blended, dipped, folded, layered, built.
- Use one strong idea per headline.
- Keep product descriptions to one or two appetite-led sentences.
- Use “non-alcoholic” wherever mojito terminology appears.
- Use “working,” “from” or “request a quote” when price is not final.
- Never invent nutrition, awards, sourcing, sustainability or dietary claims.

- Cleanse.
- Sinful.
