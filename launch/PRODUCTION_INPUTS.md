# Production Inputs Required

## Business identity

- Approved public domain and legal/brand name.
- Public phone and monitored email.
- Verified store hours, holiday process, map pin, parking and pickup instructions.

## Commerce

- Payment provider and merchant account with hosted/tokenized cards plus approved Apple Pay and Google Pay wallet support.
- POS provider and menu/order mapping.
- Missouri/local tax configuration from the appropriate professional or provider.
- Delivery boundary, minimum, fees, timing, driver/platform responsibility and failure handling.
- Approved refund, cancellation, remake, catering deposit and chargeback rules.

## Customer data

- Production database, authentication, recovery and session policy.
- Retention/deletion/export rules and privacy owner.
- Email/SMS vendors, transactional templates, consent evidence and opt-out handling.

## Products and marketing

- Approved prices, recipes, yields, suppliers and portions.
- Verified allergen controls and nutrition data.
- Final product/packaging photography and usage approval.
- Approved rewards economics, student verification and offer terms.
- Analytics/advertising tools and consent plan.

## Operations

- Error and uptime monitoring.
- Logs without unnecessary personal or payment data.
- Backup and restore test.
- On-call owner, launch window, rollback authority and customer-support scripts.
