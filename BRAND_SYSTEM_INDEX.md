## Approval boundary

Stage 02 completes the visual system required by the roadmap. It does not claim:

- Final product photography.
- Final packaging dimensions or print dielines.
- Trademark registration or clearance.
- Final menu, prices, recipes or nutrition.
- A connected payment, POS or fulfillment provider.

Those items remain assigned to later stages.
