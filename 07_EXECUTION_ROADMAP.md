# ST. JUICE — Nine-Stage Execution Roadmap

## Stage 01 — Master Foundation

Scope:

- Consolidate conversations, research, requirements and constraints.
- Preserve the full benchmark brief.
- Lock known facts and identify placeholders.
- Define menu, site, account and media architecture.

Exit gate:

- Master ZIP created and validated.
- User returns it for Stage 02.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-01-Master-Foundation.zip`

## Stage 02 — Brand System

Scope:

- Final pistachio palette and secondary colors.
- Typography.
- Logo usage and fruit icon direction.
- Buttons, cards, iconography and patterns.
- Default, Student and Business theme tokens.
- Initial packaging visual system.

Exit gate:

- Coherent brand board and reusable visual assets.
- Approved rules recorded without removing Stage 01 documents.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-02-Brand-System.zip`

## Stage 03 — Menu and Content

Scope:

- Final category list.
- Product names and descriptions.
- Working prices, sizes and modifiers.
- Build Your Mood configuration.
- Party-box and catering structure.
- Ingredient, allergen and nutrition content schema.
- Brand story and core site copy.

Exit gate:

- Menu data is structured and ready for the site.
- Every placeholder is labeled.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-03-Menu-Content.zip`

## Stage 04 — Images and Video

Scope:

- Hero art.
- Priority product images.
- Packaging mockups.
- Category art.
- Account-mode art.
- Short video/motion assets.
- Final-versus-placeholder asset manifest.

Exit gate:

- First visual review package.
- Media optimized and traceable.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-04-Images-Videos.zip`

## Stage 05 — Website Design and Front End

Scope:

- Responsive home page.
- Menu and category pages.
- Product pages.
- Build Your Mood interface.
- Drops, boxes, catering, rewards and location pages.
- Mobile navigation and account entry.

Exit gate:

- Complete visual site experience with representative content.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-05-Website-Design.zip`

## Stage 06 — Ordering and Payments

Scope:

- Cart.
- Pickup/delivery and scheduling.
- Checkout.
- Modifier pricing.
- Promotions.
- Order confirmation/status.
- Payment/POS integration layer or safe test mode until credentials are connected.

Exit gate:

- End-to-end test order succeeds.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-06-Ordering-Payments.zip`

## Stage 07 — Accounts, Rewards and Personalization

Scope:

- Guest, Regular, Student and Business/Catering experiences.
- Authentication.
- Theme and offer personalization.
- Rewards dashboard.
- Favorites, saved mixes and reorder.
- Student verification interface.
- Catering account tools.

Exit gate:

- Each account path works without breaking the shared catalog or checkout.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-07-Accounts-Rewards.zip`

## Stage 08 — Final QA, Legal, SEO and Performance

Scope:

- Mobile, desktop and accessibility QA.
- Performance optimization.
- SEO and local structured data.
- Analytics events.
- Privacy, terms, refund, cookie, SMS and accessibility content.
- Allergen and nutrition verification state.
- Remove unintended placeholders.

Exit gate:

- Launch checklist passes.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-08-Final-QA.zip`

## Stage 09 — Launch Ready

Scope:

- Connect domain.
- Connect real payments/POS.
- Confirm real phone, email, fees, tax and fulfillment settings.
- Connect analytics and business profiles.
- Run a real controlled order test.
- Publish the approved site.

Exit gate:

- Public website available and operational.
- Final source and launch package preserved.

ZIP: `ST-JUICE-09-Launch-Ready.zip`

## Review cadence

Only three major review points are required unless the user requests more:

1. After Stage 04: brand, imagery and first visual direction.
2. After Stage 07: complete functional and personalized experience.
3. After Stage 09: final public launch.

The remaining stages may proceed continuously without requiring the user to say “continue” after every internal task.

## Preservation rule

Each ZIP is a full cumulative package. A later ZIP must include all approved work from earlier stages. The returned ZIP is the only accepted starting point for the next stage.
