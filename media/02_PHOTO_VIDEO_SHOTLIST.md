# ST. JUICE — Real Photography and Video Shot List

## Purpose

This is the replacement plan for Stage 04 concept media. No generated product or packaging visual becomes factual merely because it appears in the website prototype.

## Preproduction gate

Before camera day, lock:

- Approved recipe cards, yields and portions.
- Final vessels, lids, straws, plates, liners and boxes.
- Exact garnish and topping rules.
- Approved menu names and current prices.
- Food-safe hold times and delivery presentation.
- Store access, model/property releases and working surfaces.
- Shot ownership, music license if any and deliverable rights.

- Regular: one person ordering, receiving, enjoying and reordering; phone screen must be composited from approved UI.

## Hero-loop edit plan

1. Pistachio pour, 0.0–1.0 s.
2. Chocolate drizzle, 1.0–2.0 s.
3. Pistachio finishing, 2.0–3.0 s.
4. Fruit assembly, 3.0–4.0 s.
5. Box unboxing, 4.0–5.2 s.
6. Final product tableau and seamless hold, 5.2–7.0 s.

Deliver silent-loop WebM and MP4, 1920×1080 and mobile-safe vertical/near-square variants. Never autoplay audio. Provide pause behavior for essential motion and honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Final acceptance checklist

- Recipe and portion match approved operations.
- Packaging, labels and counts match fulfillment reality.
- No third-party marks or unlicensed art appear.
- Releases and licenses are filed.
- Color is consistent across the product set.
- Crops preserve recognizable product scale.
- Alt-text notes are written.
- Every video has a still fallback.
- Compressed files pass visual and performance review.
