# ST. JUICE — Media Production, Rights and Launch Rules

## Provenance statement

The Stage 04 raster concepts were created as original AI-generated art direction using the built-in image-generation workflow. The animated and static SVG assets were created originally for ST. JUICE. The category tiles and WebP files are mechanical crops/derivatives of those Stage 04 sources.

No image or video from Juice Time, Chocolate DIP, the seven benchmark sites, Saint Louis University or any other competitor/university was copied, downloaded, stripped of branding, recolored or relabeled as ST. JUICE.

## Allowed Stage 04 use

- Internal visual review.
- Website layout and component development.
- Prelaunch prototype or private demonstration where placeholder status is controlled.
- Photography/video preproduction reference.
- Owner discussion of packaging direction.

## Not approved by Stage 04

- Treating generated food as a verified recipe, portion or ingredient statement.
- Treating generated packaging as vendor-ready, food-contact approved or dimensionally correct.
- Using generated people as evidence of a real customer, employee, partnership or endorsement.
- Implying university sponsorship or official student-program affiliation.
- Publishing generated concepts as documentary store/product photography without explicit final approval.
- Claiming nutrition, allergen, halal, vegan, gluten-free, organic, sustainability or recyclability facts.

## Source-status taxonomy

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `concept_placeholder` | Directional asset; may support design but not factual product claims |
| `derivative_placeholder` | Optimized/cropped version that inherits its source's placeholder status |
| `original_motion_prototype` | Original code-native decorative asset; may advance after review |
| `approved_final` | Reserved for a future owner-approved asset with rights and truth checks complete |

## Final-photography rights file

For every final asset, record:

- Photographer/videographer or licensed source.
- Capture or license date.
- Owner/licensee and permitted channels/territories/duration.
- Model and property releases.
- Music/SFX license for video.
- Product/recipe version shown.
- Packaging/dieline version shown.
- Approval date and approver.
- Source master, derivatives and replacement history.

## Release gate by asset type

| Type | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Product photography | Approved recipe/portion, real vessel, source ownership and owner sign-off |
| Lifestyle photography | Model/property releases plus product truth review |
| Packaging photography/mockup | Vendor dieline/material confirmation and required label/legal review |
| Stock photography | License covers website, paid ads, social, duration and territory |
| Video | Footage rights, releases, music/SFX rights, still fallback and accessibility controls |
| Generated concept | Explicit owner approval plus factual-risk assessment; replacement remains preferred |
| Abstract SVG | Originality, brand, accessibility, browser and performance review |

## Metadata and handoff

- Preserve an uncompressed or high-quality source master.
- Keep web exports stripped of unnecessary metadata unless rights/credit terms require it.
- Do not infer ownership from possession of a file.
- Do not overwrite a final asset silently; use versioned filenames and update the manifest.
- The machine-readable manifest controls status. A file move, rename or web conversion never changes approval.

## Legal note

This register is an operational rights checklist, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel. Final public use should be reviewed against actual licenses, releases, claims, privacy obligations and local law.
