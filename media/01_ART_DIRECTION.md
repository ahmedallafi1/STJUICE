# ST. JUICE — Stage 04 Art Direction

## Visual promise

Freshness in daylight, controlled appetite at night. Pistachio identifies the brand; cream creates space; charcoal supplies contrast; chrome is a restrained premium detail. Fruit, chocolate and toppings provide the energy.

## Core production rules

- Product is the largest visual subject.
- Keep backgrounds controlled and free of unrelated brand marks.
- Preserve believable portion, ingredient and vessel scale in final photography.
- Use one dominant light direction and consistent color temperature across a product set.
- Maintain copy-safe space on hero images.
- Do not place critical ordering copy inside an image.
- Do not use generated images to substantiate ingredient, nutrition, quantity, packaging or offer claims.
- Every motion asset requires a still fallback and reduced-motion behavior.

## Hero composition

- Ratio: 16:9.
- Desktop: product tableau occupies the right 48–55%; left side remains quiet for headline and calls to action.
- Mobile: use the 960 WebP as a background crop or place the image below copy; never crop the main products unpredictably behind text.
- Dark mode is the primary hero expression.
- Current hero is a design placeholder and must not be treated as a verified product lineup.

## Account-mode direction

| Mode | Scene | Palette behavior | Prohibited implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | Individual drink + dessert, rewards/favorites mood | Pistachio, cream, charcoal | Guaranteed rewards value before rules are approved |
| Student | Young adults studying and sharing | Add royal blue and yellow | University sponsorship, official campus marks or eligibility claim |
| Business/Catering | Meeting table, carrier and catering box | Add muted gold and charcoal | Guaranteed delivery capacity, rates or service level |

## Packaging direction

The generated lineup shows shape, hierarchy and palette only. It intentionally omits readable claims, regulated copy, supplier dielines and logos. Final production requires measured vessels, label areas, closures, food-contact materials, vendor tolerances, sustainability verification and physical proofs.

## Web image behavior

- Stage 05 should load optimized WebP derivatives by default.
- Retain natural aspect ratios and declare dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Use descriptive alt text for meaningful product images; use empty alt text for purely decorative compositions.
- Do not put account, product or offer information only in an image.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold media; do not lazy-load the primary hero when it is the largest-content candidate.
