## Usage ratio

For a typical default page:

- 55% cream and white breathing space.
- 20% product photography or product color.
- 15% pistachio.
- 8% charcoal.
- 2% chrome or special accent.

This is a directional balance, not a pixel-count rule.

## Accessibility pairings

Approved:

- Night Charcoal on Saint Pistachio.
- Night Charcoal on Soft Cream.
- Soft Cream on Night Charcoal.
- White on Campus Blue.
- White on Pistachio Deep.
- Soft Cream on Reserve Gold only for large display text; use Night Charcoal for normal-sized text.

Not approved:

- White body text on Saint Pistachio.
- Chrome text on cream.
- Study Yellow text on white.
- Color alone as the only error, selection or dietary signal.

## Print handling

- Match Saint Pistachio by physical proof, not screen appearance alone.
- Use uncoated cream stock when feasible for warmth; confirm grease and condensation resistance.
- Metallic silver is optional foil/ink, never simulated gray when the premium effect is essential.
- One-color packaging may use charcoal-only or pistachio-deep artwork.
