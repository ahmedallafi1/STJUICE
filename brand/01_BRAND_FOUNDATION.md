# ST. JUICE — Brand Foundation

## Position

ST. JUICE is a premium juice, dessert and chocolate destination built for morning freshness, evening indulgence and shareable social moments. It should feel distinctly St. Louis: confident, warm, current and locally ownable.

## Brand idea

**Fresh energy. Sweet mood.**

This phrase is the internal creative compass, not a legally cleared public tagline. It balances the two halves of the business:

- Fresh: fruit, juice, ingredients, morning energy and visible preparation.
- Mood: dessert, chocolate, customization, social sharing and nighttime treats.

## Personality

1. **Fresh, not clinical** — ingredient-led without looking like a medical wellness brand.
2. **Indulgent, not messy** — rich desserts framed with precision and restraint.
3. **Premium, not distant** — polished enough for gifting, friendly enough for students.
4. **Expressive, not childish** — color and motion with control.
5. **Local, not borrowed** — no copied franchise, fashion-brand or university identity.

## Brand behaviors

- Make the product the visual hero.
- Use pistachio as recognition, not as a flood on every surface.
- Pair freshness with texture: citrus, seeds, swirls, drizzle and crunch.
- Let account modes alter emphasis, never the fundamental identity.
- Keep copy short, confident and appetite-led.

## Voice

### Core tone

- Direct.
- Warm.
- Slightly playful.
- Sensory.
- Never over-explained.

### Example language

- "Pick your base. Set the mood."
- "Pressed bright. Poured cold."
- "Chocolate first. Questions later."
- "Built for the whole table."

### Avoid

- Unsupported health promises.
- Forced youth slang.
- Luxury clichés such as “world-class” or “exclusive” without proof.
- Copy that imitates a competitor's catchphrases.
- University language that implies an official relationship.

## Experience principle

The system must move smoothly from product discovery to ordering. Decorative choices are successful only when category, price, modifiers, allergens, fulfillment and the next action remain obvious.
