# ST. JUICE — Typography System

## Final pair

### Display: Fraunces

Use for:

- Hero headlines.
- Campaign names.
- Drop titles.
- Packaging statements.
- Short editorial numbers.

Weights: 600 and 700.  
Style: soft optical character, controlled contrast, no excessive italics.

### Interface and body: Manrope

Use for:

- Navigation.
- Product names and prices.
- Body copy.
- Buttons and forms.
- Account, rewards and checkout UI.

Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700 and 800.

Both families are intended to be self-hosted under their applicable open-font licenses in Stage 05. Until files are bundled, system fallbacks must remain functional.

## Case and spacing

- Headings: sentence case by default.
- Wordmark: uppercase `ST. JUICE` with intentional tracking.
- Buttons: sentence case; never all-caps body-sized buttons.
- Eyebrows: uppercase Manrope 700 with `0.12em` tracking.
- Prices: tabular numerals when supported.

## Content rules

- Keep display lines under roughly 12 words.
- Avoid more than three centered text lines.
- Product descriptions remain left-aligned on menu and checkout surfaces.
- Minimum interactive and body size is 16 px on mobile forms.
- Do not replace words with icons when comprehension or accessibility suffers.
