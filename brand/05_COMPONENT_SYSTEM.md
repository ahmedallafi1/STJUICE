# ST. JUICE — Component System

## Shape language

- Standard card radius: 20 px.
- Compact control radius: 14 px.
- Filter and badge radius: 999 px.
- Border: 1 px chrome or 1 px charcoal at low opacity.
- Shadow: soft and short; never a heavy floating-dashboard shadow.

## Buttons

### Primary

- Saint Pistachio background.
- Night Charcoal text.
- 48 px minimum height; 52 px preferred for ordering.
- Hover: Pistachio 300 with 2 px lift.
- Focus: 3 px Campus Blue outline with 2 px offset.

### Dark

- Night Charcoal background.
- Soft Cream text.
- Used for checkout, evening promos and high-contrast surfaces.

### Secondary

- Transparent background.
- 1 px Night Charcoal border.
- Night Charcoal text.

### Text action

- Underline on hover and keyboard focus.
- Arrow may support the label but never replace it.

## Product card

Required fields:

- Image or honest placeholder.
- Product name.
- Short sensory description.
- Price or “From” price.
- Dietary/allergen tags when verified.
- Quick Add or Customize action.

The entire card may be clickable, but the action must still have an explicit accessible name.

## Offer card

- One dominant message.
- One eligibility label.
- One end condition or terms link.
- One primary action.
- Accent color follows the selected account mode.

## Badges

- Best Seller: pistachio tint + charcoal.
- New: charcoal + cream.
- Student: blue + white.
- Catering: gold tint + charcoal.
- Sold Out: neutral gray + explicit text.
- Allergen/dietary: outlined; no unverified claims.

## Iconography

- 24 px grid.
- 1.8 px rounded stroke.
- Open, simple forms.
- Label every unfamiliar or standalone interactive icon.
- Use the supplied originals for account, bag, rewards, pickup, delivery and catering.

## Pattern

The Seed Orbit pattern is a supporting texture built from the logo seeds. Use it:

- At 3–8% visual strength behind cream or pistachio fields.
- On tissue, cup sleeves, box interiors and section dividers.
- Cropped generously rather than tiled densely.

Never place the pattern behind small text or critical order controls.

## Motion

- Hover/focus transitions: 160 ms.
- Card lift and panel transitions: 220 ms.
- Account-theme transition: 260 ms.
- Use ease-out; no elastic or continuous decorative motion.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
