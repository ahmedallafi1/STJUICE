# ST. JUICE — Initial Packaging System

## Concept: Quiet outside, mood inside

The exterior uses a disciplined pistachio, cream and charcoal system. Opening the package reveals Seed Orbit patterning and a controlled product color. This makes everyday service recognizable while keeping drops and gift boxes collectible.

## Packaging families

### Cold cups

- Clear or frosted body so product color remains visible.
- Charcoal Saint Slice mark.
- Pistachio information band or sleeve.
- Drink name and modifier label remain legible.

### Bottles

- Cream or clear label.
- Pistachio top band.
- Large product name, small ingredient and storage block.
- Batch/date area reserved; no placeholder health claims.

### Dessert boxes

- Cream exterior.
- Charcoal wordmark.
- Pistachio seal.
- Seed Orbit interior.
- Product-specific color restricted to a removable flavor label.

### Cup carriers and takeout bags

- Kraft or cream substrate.
- One-color charcoal artwork for cost control.
- Pistachio sticker or handle detail.
- Safety and condensation performance take priority over decorative cutouts.

### Party and catering boxes

- Charcoal exterior for premium distinction.
- Cream wordmark.
- Reserve Gold event label.
- Pistachio interior freshness cue.
- Tamper seal and order-identification zone.

## Label information hierarchy

1. Product name.
2. Size or serving count.
3. Key modifiers.
4. Customer/order name.
5. Prepared date/time when operationally required.
6. Storage or reheating instruction.
7. Ingredients/allergens after verification.

## Print system

- Base run: one-color charcoal on cream/kraft.
- Standard branded run: charcoal + pistachio.
- Premium run: charcoal + pistachio + metallic silver or gold.
- Drops change labels, tissue or sleeves—not the core package architecture.

## Vendor validation required later

- Food-contact certification.
- Grease, heat, freezer and condensation resistance.
- Tamper evidence.
- Cup/lid compatibility.
- Delivery stack stability.
- Recyclability claims.
- Minimum order quantities and print tolerances.
