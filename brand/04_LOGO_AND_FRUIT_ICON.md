# ST. JUICE — Logo and Fruit Icon

## Final direction: Saint Slice

The primary symbol is **Saint Slice**, an original abstract dragon-fruit cross-section. A rounded outer slice communicates fruit and softness; the leaf crown signals freshness; the seed orbit gives the mark energy without using a face, mascot or copied fashion symbol.

The mark is deliberately simple enough for:

- Cup stamps.
- App icon and favicon.
- Social avatar.
- Bottle cap.
- Dessert seal.
- Store vinyl.
- Embroidery and one-color print.

## Logo suite

- `st-juice-fruit-mark.svg` — mark only.
- `st-juice-wordmark.svg` — wordmark only.
- `st-juice-lockup-horizontal.svg` — default digital and storefront lockup.
- `st-juice-lockup-stacked.svg` — square and centered applications.

## Default treatments

1. Night Charcoal on Soft Cream.
2. Night Charcoal on Saint Pistachio.
3. Soft Cream on Night Charcoal.
4. One-color charcoal or one-color cream for constrained production.

Product-color versions of the mark are campaign artwork, not the permanent master logo.

## Clear space

Use the fruit mark's center seed width as the minimum clear space on every side. Do not place type, borders, other marks or crop edges inside this area.

## Minimum size

- Fruit mark: 20 px digital; 8 mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: 120 px digital; 32 mm print.
- Stacked lockup: 72 px digital; 22 mm print.

Below those sizes, use the simplified mark and remove the small seed details if reproduction becomes muddy.

## Wordmark treatment

- Uppercase `ST. JUICE`.
- Strong geometric construction.
- Slightly expanded tracking.
- The period is mandatory.
- Do not substitute “Saint Juice” in the logo.
- Body typography does not imitate the wordmark.

## Never

- Stretch, rotate or outline the logo.
- Put the logo on visually noisy photography without a solid field.
- Add a face to the fruit mark.
- Replace the seed orbit with a cross or fashion-brand symbol.
- Recolor the permanent logo in official university colors as if co-branded.
- Use unofficial shadows, bevels, chrome effects or gradients inside the master logo.

## Production note

The supplied SVGs are Stage 02 reusable masters. Before large-format signage or trademark filing, a professional identity review should convert final wordmark typography to outlines, check small-size reproduction and complete a trademark clearance search.
