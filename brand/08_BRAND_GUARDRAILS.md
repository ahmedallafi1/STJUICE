# ST. JUICE — Brand Guardrails

## Identity protection

- Use original ST. JUICE artwork only.
- Do not copy Juice Time, Chocolate DIP, Chrome Hearts, SLU or any other brand's logo, protected type treatment, photography or trade dress.
- Do not imply sponsorship, partnership or university affiliation.
- Complete trademark clearance before final filing, large signage production or franchise rollout.

## Image integrity

- Never remove another brand's logo from an image and replace it with ST. JUICE.
- Generated imagery must be labeled as concept/placeholder when it could be mistaken for a real product or store.
- Final menu photography must match the sold portion, packaging and ingredient presentation.

## Claims

- No calorie, protein, sugar, organic, vegan, gluten-free or allergen-free claim without verified data and cross-contact procedures.
- “Fresh” must describe the actual preparation and storage process.
- Student discounts require eligibility rules and terms.
- Offer urgency must be truthful.

## Accessibility

- Maintain WCAG-aligned contrast.
- Keep visible focus states.
- Pair status colors with icons and text.
- Provide alt text for informative imagery.
- Decorative Seed Orbit patterns receive empty alt text or CSS background treatment.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Logo governance

- Use the supplied master files.
- Keep the period in ST. JUICE.
- Keep the logo clear of dense photography.
- Do not add outlines, bevels, faux chrome, shadows or unapproved gradients.
- Do not turn the Saint Slice into a character.

## Packaging governance

- Do not send concept SVGs directly to print as dielines.
- Confirm every manufacturing measurement with the vendor.
- Preserve required legal, allergen, handling and recycling space.
- Use removable labels for short-lived drops instead of wasting permanent packaging.

## Change control

A later stage may extend the system but should not silently change:

- Saint Pistachio.
- Fraunces + Manrope.
- Saint Slice.
- The three theme modes.
- The core cream/charcoal balance.

Any material change should be recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` and the decisions register.
