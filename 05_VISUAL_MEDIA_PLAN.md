# ST. JUICE — Visual and Media Plan

## Master visual idea

Pistachio luxury framed by cream, charcoal and restrained chrome. Product colors supply energy. The brand feels fresh in the morning and more cinematic at night without changing identity.

## Core palette roles

- Pistachio: brand recognition and primary actions.
- Cream: background, breathing room and premium warmth.
- Charcoal/black: navigation, evening sections and street-luxury contrast.
- Chrome/silver: borders, badges, icon details and high-value accents.
- Royal blue: Student mode only.
- Muted gold: Business/Catering mode only.
- Fruit colors: product- and drop-specific accents.

Exact color values and usage percentages are Stage 02 deliverables.

## Typography roles

- Display face: distinctive, premium and expressive without copying Chrome Hearts or SLU typography.
- Interface face: highly readable on mobile and suitable for prices, modifiers and legal copy.
- Maximum two primary typefaces.

- Juice pouring.
- Chocolate fountain or drizzle.
- Pistachio sauce application.
- Dubai chocolate break.
- Crepe fettuccine cutting.
- Mini pancake finishing.
- Waffle crunch or cutaway.
- Fresh fruit preparation.
- Soft-serve swirl.
- Order packing.
- Party-box unboxing.
- Customer reaction or hands sharing.
- Five-to-eight-second hero loop.

## Production rules

- Product is always the hero.
- Use controlled backgrounds.
- Maintain consistent lighting and color temperature.
- Show real scale.
- Avoid deceptive quantities or impossible textures.
- Provide a still fallback for every important video.
- Optimize media for mobile.
- Keep critical ordering content readable without video.
- Use motion to create appetite, not to delay checkout.

## Media sourcing and rights

Competitor imagery may be studied but must not be copied, downloaded, rebranded or passed off as ST. JUICE work.

Production may use:

- Original photography.
- User-provided product and store footage.
- Properly licensed stock where appropriate.
- Custom-generated concept imagery and mockups.
- Original branded animations.

Generated or placeholder visuals must be replaced when factual product appearance, portion size or packaging accuracy is required.

## Account-mode art direction

### Guest/default

- Pistachio, cream and charcoal.
- Broad best sellers and current drop.

### Regular

- Pistachio, cream, charcoal and chrome.
- Rewards, favorites and personalized recommendations.

### Student

- Pistachio, royal blue, cream and a small lemon-yellow accent.
- Study, campus-area and value-oriented scenes.
- No official university marks.

### Business/Catering

- Pistachio, charcoal, cream and muted gold.
- Box materials, unboxing, table setups and group scale.
