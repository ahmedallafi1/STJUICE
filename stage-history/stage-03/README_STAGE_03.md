# ST. JUICE — Stage 03 Menu and Content

Version: 3.0  
Stage: 03 of 09  
Status: Complete and ready for Stage 04  
Project: ST. JUICE website and digital ordering system

## Purpose

This cumulative package contains the complete Stage 01 foundation, the complete Stage 02 brand system and the Stage 03 website-ready menu/content system. It is the only accepted starting point for Stage 04.

Stage 03 delivers:

- 12 final launch categories.
- 45 primary items, including two active limited drops.
- 9 group formats for flights, liters and boxes.
- Working direct-order prices, sizes and modifiers.
- Build Your Mood configuration and compatibility rules.
- Party-box and catering products, lead times, form data and quote states.
- Ingredient, allergen and nutrition data structures.
- A public brand story and complete English site copy deck.
- Validated machine-readable JSON ready for website implementation.

- Bracketed phone, email, social and commercial terms are placeholders.
