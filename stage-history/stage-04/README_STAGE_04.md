- One six-frame motion storyboard, one original animated SVG hero loop and one static SVG fallback.
- 23 optimized WebP derivatives for direct Stage 05 implementation.
- A traceable media manifest, production shot list, prompt record and rights/usage rules.

## Truth and launch status

- Every generated PNG and its WebP derivative is a clearly labeled **concept placeholder**.
- Generated food and packaging visuals are not verified representations of final recipes, portions, vessels or boxes.
- They may be used for design review and prelaunch prototyping; factual assets must be replaced with approved production photography before public launch.
- The original abstract SVG loop may remain as final decorative motion after brand/accessibility approval because it makes no factual product claim.
- No competitor photography, video, logo or university mark was copied or rebranded.
