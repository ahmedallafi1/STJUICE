## Menu experience

- Browse by category, mood and occasion.
- Search by product or ingredient.
- Filter by dietary preference only when verified.
- Quick Add for simple products.
- Open the product page for complex customization.
- Visually separate core items from drops.
- Preserve scroll/filter state after viewing a product.

## Cart and checkout

- Edit modifiers from the cart.
- Display item-level allergen notes and special instructions.
- Suggest one or two relevant additions, never an overwhelming carousel.
- Support promo code and rewards redemption.
- Guest checkout remains available.
- Clearly show subtotal, tax, tip, delivery and other fees.
- Show pickup/delivery time before payment.
- Support scheduled orders.
- Do not require marketing consent to place an order.

## Mobile-first requirements

- Thumb-friendly navigation.
- Persistent cart visibility.
- Large tap targets.
- Collapsible filters.
- Fast images with responsive sizes.
- Video fallback image.
- No stacked popups.
- Last location and order mode remembered.
- Apple Pay/Google Pay placement when connected.
- Keyboard and screen-reader support.

## Conversion systems

- Clear hero action.
- Quick Add.
- Sticky mobile add button.
- Bundles.
- Product-level cross-sell.
- Free-delivery progress when configured.
- Limited-drop countdown only when truthful.
- Student offer surfaced after account selection.
- Rewards preview before account creation.
- Reorder and saved mixes.
- Catering lead capture.

## Local SEO and discovery

- Consistent name, address, phone and hours.
- Location page with map and service modes.
- Structured restaurant/local-business data.
- Product/menu structured data where appropriate.
- Page titles and descriptions written for St. Louis customers.
- Social preview images.
- Fast, descriptive product images and alt text.

## Performance and accessibility targets

- Mobile-first loading.
- Modern image formats.
- Lazy loading below the first viewport.
- Minimal blocking scripts.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Text contrast appropriate to accessibility standards.
- Visible focus states.
- Form errors that do not rely only on color.
- Descriptive labels for account and cart icons.
