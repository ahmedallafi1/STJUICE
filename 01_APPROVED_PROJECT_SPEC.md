# ST. JUICE — Approved Project Specification

## 1. Brand and business

- Public brand name: **ST. JUICE**.
- Legal entity context: **ST. JUICE LLC**, Missouri.
- Business concept: premium juice bar, dessert destination and chocolate experience.
- Business ambition: launch strongly in St. Louis, become locally recognizable and remain structurally ready for multiple branches and eventual franchising.
- First branch: **11 S Vandeventer Ave, St. Louis, Missouri**.
- Planning status for the website: treat the location, lease and opening as confirmed.
- Website status language: present the branch as open and able to receive orders.
- Current branch count: one.

## 2. Hours and service modes

Approved operating-hours assumption:

- Sunday–Thursday: 6:30 AM–9:00 PM.
- Friday–Saturday: 6:30 AM–11:00 PM.

Approved service modes:

- Dine-in.
- Pickup.
- Delivery.
- No dedicated private parking should be claimed.
- Parking copy should be factual and modest; never promise parking that is unavailable.

## 3. Audience

Primary audiences:

- Saint Louis University-area students.
- Young adults and social-media-driven customers.
- Nearby residents and commuters.
- Families.
- Morning customers seeking fresh juice, smoothies or protein items.
- Afternoon dessert customers.
- Evening and weekend craving traffic.
- Groups ordering party boxes.
- Offices, events and corporate catering clients.

## 4. Positioning

ST. JUICE must not look like a cold, clinical health-only juice shop or a cheap, childish dessert store.

The target balance is:

- Premium but not intimidating.
- Youthful but not childish.
- Bold but controlled.
- Photogenic but usable.
- Fresh by day and indulgent by night.
- Street-luxury energy with credible fruit and ingredient quality.

Working brand promise:

> Fresh enough for every day. Bold enough to remember.

Alternative campaign line:

> Fresh by day. Wild by night.

The final slogan remains replaceable during Stage 02, but the positioning above is locked.

## 5. Visual direction

Approved primary color: **pistachio green**.

Supporting direction:

- Soft cream for breathing space.
- Deep charcoal or black for contrast and street-luxury depth.
- Chrome or silver details for premium accents.
- Product colors provide controlled seasonal accents.
- The interface must not use every fruit color at once.

Student experience:

- Pistachio + royal blue + cream, with restrained lemon-yellow accents.
- No official university marks, seals, mascots, protected typography or statements of sponsorship.

Business/catering experience:

- Pistachio + charcoal + cream + restrained muted gold.

Known icon directions include a lemon or stylized fruit mark. Dragon fruit explorations exist, but Stage 02 must lock the final symbol without copying other brands.

## 6. Language and tone

- Website language at launch: English.
- Tone: concise, confident, vivid, modern and slightly playful.
- Product copy should create appetite without becoming childish or exaggerated.
- Technical and legal copy should be plain, precise and trustworthy.

## 7. Menu direction

The menu should combine the commercial breadth of Juice Time with the clear chocolate-and-dessert experience of Chocolate DIP, while using original ST. JUICE names, copy, images and recipes.

Required product families:

- Signature drinks.
- Fresh juices and fruit cocktails.
- Smoothies and yogurt shakes.
- Milkshakes and protein drinks.
- Mojitos and refreshers.
- Açaí bowls and fruit cups.
- Soft serve.
- Chocolate-covered fruit and fruit sticks.
- Crepes and crepe fettuccine.
- Waffles and waffle sticks.
- Mini pancakes.
- Brownies, cookies and seasonal desserts.
- Dubai chocolate collection.
- Bottles and liters.
- ST. Flights, party boxes and catering.
- Limited drops.

## 8. Commerce and ordering

Required functionality:

- Browse without an account.
- Guest checkout.
- Pickup and delivery selection.
- Scheduled orders.
- Product sizes and modifiers.
- Sauces, toppings and paid add-ons.
- Real-time price updates.
- Promo codes and offers.
- Tips, taxes and fees.
- Apple Pay, Google Pay and card support when the real processor is connected.
- Order confirmation and status communication.
- Saved favorites, reordering and saved custom mixes for signed-in customers.
- Clear out-of-stock handling and alternatives.

The real payment processor, POS, fees and merchant credentials remain launch dependencies. No password, payment credential or bank information belongs in project documents or chat.

## 9. Required major systems

- Build Your Mood customization journey.
- Limited drops.
- Rewards and tier progression.
- Student-specific experience.
- Regular customer account.
- Business/catering account.
- Party box builder.
- Catering inquiry and ordering flow.
- Gift cards.
- Nutrition and allergen information structure.
- Local branch information.
- Analytics-ready conversion funnel.
- Mobile-first interface.

## 10. Content placeholders approved for early stages

Until verified details are provided, the project may use clearly marked professional placeholders for:

- Public phone number.
- Customer-service email.
- Catering email.
- Social URLs.
- Custom domain.
- Product prices.
- Exact preparation times.
- Delivery radius and fees.
- POS/payment provider.
- Verified nutrition figures.

Placeholders must never be presented as verified legal, nutritional or payment facts in the public launch build.
