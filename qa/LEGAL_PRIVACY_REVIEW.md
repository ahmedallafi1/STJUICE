# Legal, Privacy & Content Review

Stage 08 supplies customer-readable draft routes while keeping every unapproved promise explicit.

Owner review is required for:

- legal business identity and public contact;
- order acceptance, cancellation, refund, remake and delivery terms;
- catering quotes, deposits, minimums, rescheduling and cancellation;
- promotions, gift cards, tips, rewards, birthdays and student pricing;
- marketing email/SMS consent, opt-out and quiet-hour practices;
- production vendors, data uses, retention, deletion, access and security;
- cookie/analytics/advertising consent requirements;
- governing law, disputes, liability limitations and age/capacity terms;
- allergen, nutrition, ingredient, health and sustainability claims.

The included drafts are operational scaffolding, not legal advice or publication approval.
