# ST. JUICE — Pricing, Sizes and Modifiers

## Status

Every value in this document is a working planning price. Before launch, ownership must approve prices after food-cost, packaging, labor, tax, merchant fee and delivery-platform review.

## Pricing rules

- One included sauce/topping means the first selection is $0 and later compatible selections use the add-on price.
- The cart shows modifier price changes immediately.
- “From” appears only when valid configurations have different prices.
- Delivery-platform price differences must be transparent.
- Tax, delivery, service and tip are never folded invisibly into a product price.
- Promo codes cannot create a negative line item or reduce below configured margin floors.
- Student and business offers must have written eligibility and terms.

## Cancellation and substitution content

- If a fruit is unavailable, show compatible alternatives before checkout.
- A substitution that adds an allergen requires customer approval.
- Out-of-stock modifiers must disappear or show unavailable; they must not silently substitute.
- Scheduled catering prices are held only for the period defined in the quote terms.

## Approval checklist

Before moving `priceStatus` from `working_requires_approval` to `approved`:

1. Recipe and portion are tested.
2. Ingredient and packaging costs are loaded.
3. Labor and waste assumptions are reviewed.
4. Gross margin is reviewed by channel.
5. Sales tax and fees are configured.
6. Owner approves direct and third-party prices.
