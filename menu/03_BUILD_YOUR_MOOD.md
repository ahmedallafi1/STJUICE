# ST. JUICE — Build Your Mood

## Promise

**Pick your base. Set the mood.**

Build Your Mood is one guided configurator, not a list of separate “build your own” products. It displays only compatible choices, updates price live and explains allergen impact before Add to Order.

## Compatibility rules

- Fresh juice excludes dairy, soft serve, chocolate sauce and dry crumb unless explicitly tested.
- Açaí is not a Stage 03 custom base; customers customize the prepared bowl products.
- Protein appears only for smoothie/yogurt bases.
- Hot crepe, waffle and pancake bases cannot be configured as delivery-unrestricted; show quality guidance.
- Pistachio options always add a tree-nut allergen flag.
- Chocolate and cookie/brownie options may add milk, soy, wheat and egg flags based on supplier labels.
- Kataifi adds wheat/gluten and may add additional supplier allergens.
- Any substitution that changes allergens forces a new confirmation.

## Naming saved mixes

- Maximum 30 characters.
- Filter profanity and impersonation.
- Saved names are private by default.
- Public/community sharing is out of launch scope.
