# ST. JUICE — Party Boxes and Catering

## Customer paths

### Order-now boxes

Designed for same-day or scheduled ordering within normal capacity:

- Study Night Box.
- Date Night Box.
- Game Day Box.
- Birthday Box.
- Build Your Party Box, within configured limits.

### Catering request

Required for larger quantities, staffed service, delivery outside the standard zone, custom branding, business billing or event-specific setup.

## Lead times

- Order-now boxes: show earliest real slot from store capacity.
- 8–20 guests: request at least 24 hours.
- 21–50 guests: request at least 48 hours.
- 51+ guests, staffed service or custom branding: request at least 5 business days.

Lead times are working operational rules and require manager confirmation.

## Safety and fulfillment rules

- Hot, frozen and chocolate-dipped items need channel-specific holding limits.
- Delivery packaging must separate hot and cold items.
- Every group order includes an allergen/cross-contact notice.
- Individual labels are recommended for office orders.
- Custom branding cannot imply a partnership without authorization.
- Deposits, cancellation windows, gratuity, delivery fees and tax remain placeholders until approved.
