## Activation gate

A deferred item enters the live catalog only after:

1. Recipe and portion test.
2. Food-cost and price approval.
3. Supplier and allergen review.
4. Packaging and delivery-quality test.
5. Photography status is assigned.
6. One existing item is retired or the category-size limit is intentionally raised.

Machine-readable status is in `data/deferred-catalog.json`.
