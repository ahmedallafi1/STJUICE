# ST. JUICE — Account and Personalization System

## Principle

ST. JUICE uses one commerce platform and one menu source. Account type changes theme, merchandising, offers and tools without duplicating the entire website.

## Entry experience

The account icon opens a clear selector:

- Continue as Guest.
- Regular Account.
- Student Account.
- Business & Catering.

Customers must be able to understand the brand and browse the menu before choosing an account type.

## 1. Guest

Capabilities:

- Browse full public menu.
- Choose pickup or delivery.
- Customize products.
- Checkout without creating an account.
- Receive order confirmation.
- See the value of rewards without being blocked by signup.

Theme:

- Pistachio + cream + charcoal.

## 2. Regular Account

Capabilities:

- Rewards balance and tier.
- Birthday reward.
- Favorites.
- Saved addresses.
- Order history.
- One-click reorder.
- Saved Build Your Mood mixes.
- Early-access eligibility.
- Referral rewards.
- Personalized but explainable recommendations.

Theme:

- Pistachio + cream + charcoal + chrome.

## 3. Student Account

Capabilities:

- Student-only offers.
- Study Night Boxes.
- Campus-area drops.
- Eligible double-points windows.
- Student value bundles.
- Student events and voting.
- All Regular Account features.

Theme:

- Pistachio + royal blue + cream + restrained lemon yellow.

Verification planning:

- School email or a later approved verification method.
- Verification status and expiration must be visible.
- No official SLU affiliation should be implied.
- Student discounts require clear eligibility and terms.

## 4. Business & Catering

Capabilities:

- Catering catalog.
- Party and office boxes.
- Scheduled orders.
- Repeating orders.
- Corporate gifts.
- Quote requests.
- Contact and delivery details saved at business level.
- Bulk pricing only when configured.
- Order history and invoices/receipts when supported.

Theme:

- Pistachio + charcoal + cream + muted gold.

## Dynamic changes by account type

The platform may change:

- Color accents.
- Announcement-bar message.
- Hero campaign.
- Featured categories.
- Best-seller order.
- Eligible prices or offers.
- Bundle recommendations.
- Rewards messaging.
- Navigation shortcuts.

The platform must not silently change:

- Base product facts.
- Allergens.
- Fees.
- Taxes.
- Availability.
- Legal terms.

## Rewards framework

Working tiers:

- Fresh.
- Gold.
- Saint.
- Icon.

Possible benefits:

- Points per dollar.
- Birthday product.
- Drop bonuses.
- Early access.
- Free or discounted add-on.
- Referral reward.
- Monthly challenges.
- Student-specific bonuses.
- Vote on a future drop.

Exact earning rate, redemption value, expiration and financial liability require approval before launch.

## Privacy principles

- Guest checkout remains available.
- Collect only data necessary for the selected feature.
- Marketing SMS and email consent must be separate from transactional communication.
- Account type must not be used for hidden discriminatory pricing.
- Student verification data should be minimized.
- Users need access to account, consent and deletion controls.
- Saved payment methods depend on the selected payment provider and should not be stored directly by ST. JUICE application code.

## Recommended routes

- `/account`
- `/student`
- `/rewards`
- `/catering`

These are experiences within one system, not separate independently maintained websites.
